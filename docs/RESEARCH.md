# Research notes (verified 2026-09-27)

Every number on a slide comes from this file. Each fact was checked against a
primary source (arXiv abstract or HTML, official blog, model card, official
repo). **[D]** marks a number derived from a formula in the paper rather than
printed in it.

## Seeing: patches and CLIP

**ViT** (Dosovitskiy et al. 2020, arXiv:2010.11929)
- "An Image is Worth 16x16 Words". N = HW/P² patches, plus a learnable [class] token.
- [D] 224 × 224 with P = 16: 14 × 14 = **196** patches (+ CLS = 197).
- ViT-Base hidden size D = 768 (Table 1).

**CLIP** (Radford et al. 2021, arXiv:2103.00020)
- **400M** (image, text) pairs from the internet. Contrastive objective. Minibatch **32,768**.
- Zero-shot ImageNet **76.2%**, matching the original supervised ResNet-50
  "without using any of the 1.28 million crowd-labeled training examples".
  This figure belongs to the best CLIP model, not a CLIP ResNet-50.

**SigLIP** (Zhai et al. 2023, arXiv:2303.15343)
- Pairwise sigmoid loss, which needs no global normalisation over the batch.
  32k batch "sufficient". (Not on a slide; for Q&A.)

## The bridge

**LLaVA** (Liu et al. 2023, arXiv:2304.08485)
- CLIP ViT-L/14, then a trainable **linear** projection W, then Vicuna.
- Stage 1: CC3M filtered to **595K** pairs, only W trained, under **4 h**.
- Stage 2: **158K** instruction samples generated by language-only GPT-4
  (58K conversation, 23K description, 77K reasoning), under **10 h**. Hardware: **8 × A100**.

**LLaVA-1.5** (Liu et al. 2023, arXiv:2310.03744)
- Two-layer **MLP** projector, CLIP-ViT-L-336px. About 1 day on one 8-A100 node.
- [D] (336 / 14)² = 24² = **576** visual tokens.

**BLIP-2** (Li et al. 2023, arXiv:2301.12597)
- Frozen image encoder + frozen LLM, joined by a **Q-Former** (**188M** params,
  **32** queries of dim 768).

**Flamingo** (Alayrac et al. 2022, arXiv:2204.14198)
- **80B**, built on a frozen 70B Chinchilla.
- Section 2: "We freeze the pretrained LM blocks, and insert gated
  cross-attention dense blocks between the original layers, trained from
  scratch." A Perceiver Resampler produces 64 visual outputs.

**PaliGemma** (Beyer et al. 2024, arXiv:2407.07726)
- SigLIP-So400m + Gemma-2B. "Full (unmasked) attention on the whole input, i.e.
  the image and prefix tokens"; the suffix is autoregressive (the `answer` mask).

## Token budget

- **Qwen2-VL** (arXiv:2409.12191): Naive Dynamic Resolution, a variable number
  of visual tokens per image. M-RoPE.
- **SmolVLM** (Marafioti et al. 2025, arXiv:2504.05299):
  - Pixel shuffle with r = 4 on the small models, so **16×** fewer tokens.
  - SmolVLM-**256M** uses **under 1 GB** of GPU memory and "outperforms the
    300-times larger Idefics-80B".
- [D] 672 px at 14 px patches: 48² = 2,304 tokens. Folded 4 × 4: 12² = 144.
  This is an illustration of the ÷16, not a specific model's configuration.

## Limits

- **POPE** (Li et al. 2023, arXiv:2305.10355): yes/no object probes. Hallucinated
  objects are disproportionately frequent or co-occurring ones.
- **MMVP / Eyes Wide Shut** (Tong et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.06209):
  - "CLIP-blind pairs" are images CLIP embeds as similar despite clear visual differences.
  - There are 9 patterns, including *Orientation and Direction* and *Quantity and Count*.
  - Humans score about **96%**. Most models score below random chance (25%),
    and even GPT-4V trails humans by more than 50 points.
- The limits slide's answers are illustrative; the failure modes are the papers'.

## From VLM to robot (chapter 04)

- **RT-2** (Brohan et al. 2023, arXiv:2307.15818): "Vision-Language-Action Models
  Transfer Web Knowledge to Robotic Control".
  - An action is a text string of **8** tokens: terminate, Δpos x/y/z, Δrot x/y/z, gripper.
  - The continuous dimensions are cut into **256 uniform bins**. PaLI-X reuses
    its integer tokens; PaLM-E **overwrites the 256 least-used tokens**.
  - Emergent reasoning with chain of thought: "which object to pick up for use
    as an improvised hammer (a rock)" (the `vla-loop` example).
  - Speed: 55B at **1–3 Hz**, 5B around 5 Hz.
- **OpenVLA** (Kim et al. 2024, arXiv:2406.09246): **7B**.
  - Built on the Prismatic-7B VLM: SigLIP + DINOv2 encoder, 2-layer MLP, Llama 2 7B.
  - Each action dimension goes into **256 bins** spanning the **1st to 99th
    quantile** of the training actions.
  - The bins "overwrite the 256 least used tokens in the Llama tokenizer's
    vocabulary (which corresponds to the last 256 tokens)". [D] Llama's
    vocabulary is 32,000, so the ids are **31744–31999**.
  - About **6 Hz** on one RTX 4090.
  - `vla-same` shows 7 dimensions (6 deltas + gripper). The values are illustrative.
- **SmolVLA** (Shukor et al. 2025, arXiv:2506.01844): **450M**, SmolVLM2 backbone,
  keeps the first 16 of 32 LLM layers ("half its layers"), plus a flow-matching
  action expert (continuous, no bins). Runs on a consumer GPU or a laptop.

## Recent open VLMs (for Q&A, not on slides)

- Molmo / PixMo (arXiv:2409.17146, Sep 2024): open data, 2D pointing.
- InternVL3.5 (arXiv:2508.18265, Aug 2025): dense 1B to 38B and MoE up to 241B-A28B.
- Qwen3-VL (arXiv:2511.21631, Nov 2025): dense 2B to 32B and MoE up to 235B-A22B, SigLIP-2 encoder.
- Gemma 3 (arXiv:2503.19786, Mar 2025): 1B to 27B, adds vision.
- MMMU (arXiv:2311.16502): human experts score 76.2 to 88.6 on validation, and GPT-4V scored 56.8 at launch.
